Business Continuity & Cybersecurity

IT Backups: Your Business Survival Plan

A proper backup is not an optional "nice-to-have" — it's your IT survival plan. Ransomware, drive failure, accidental deletion by an employee, stolen laptop, or fire... disasters happen more often than expected.

Why It's Critical for Small Businesses

Many business owners neglect backup strategies thinking "it only happens to others"... until data disappears. With over 25 years of crisis recovery experience, I build foolproof backup plans so you sleep peacefully.

What I Provide for Your Backups

  • Audit of Current Solutions Is your current backup reliable? Monitored? Tested regularly? Immutable against ransomware attacks?
  • Tailored Options Local (NAS, external drives), Cloud (AWS S3, Backblaze, Wasabi, Google Workspace, OneDrive for Business), or hybrid.
  • 3-2-1 Backup Rule Implementation Strict enforcement of industry standards: 3 copies of data, 2 different media types, 1 offsite immutable copy.
  • Monitoring & Restoration Testing Automated status reports, anomaly alerts, and routine restoration test simulations (every 3 to 6 months).
  • Recovery Plan (RTO / RPO) Realistic Recovery Time (RTO) and Point (RPO) targets aligned with your operational budget and tolerance.
  • Continuous Re-evaluation As your computer fleet grows, we revise the strategy to prevent newly added workstations from being left unbacked up.
