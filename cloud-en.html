Technological Optimization & Security

Tailored Hybrid Cloud & Infrastructure

The Cloud does not have to mean overpriced monthly subscriptions or forfeiting your privacy. I help you get the most out of modern technology by combining Cloud power with the security of your own hardware.

🔒 Secure Remote Access (Zero Trust)

Forget slow and complex traditional VPNs. Using modern solutions like Tailscale or Twingate, connect your workstations, servers, and mobile devices in an end-to-end encrypted virtual private network accessible from anywhere without opening public ports to the Internet.

🐳 Virtualization & Containers (Docker)

With Docker, a single repurposed PC can host dozens of isolated, secure, lightweight applications. It's the ideal solution for running internal management tools without slowing down your primary operating system.

📂 Network Storage & Backups (NAS)

Turn an older desktop or dedicated NAS enclosure into a private network storage server. An economical alternative to centralize documents, automate backups, and retain total control over confidential data.

🤖 Pro Automation & Smart Workspace

Deploy a Raspberry Pi or mini-PC to centralize office automation (smart lighting, heating, cameras, or locks) via open platforms like Home Assistant or n8n.

My Hybrid Cloud Philosophy

I favor proven, open, and secure solutions that maximize the lifespan of your existing hardware while leveraging Cloud reliability for critical workloads. Avoid unnecessary recurring fees while keeping enterprise-grade infrastructure.
